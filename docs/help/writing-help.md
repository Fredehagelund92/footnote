Paste a message, a link or a few words, and writing help writes the context item as your draft, a field at a time, in a panel beside the editor. You check each field, and you send or publish it yourself.

## Before you start

Writing help is off until an admin turns it on, in **Settings**, **Advanced**, under Writing help. It needs an AI model, the one set for Ask: with none connected, Write context fills the fields by rule, as **Fill it in** does. It is on a computer or tablet, not on a phone. [Write context](write-context.md) says how a draft is sent or published.

> **What it sends.** With writing help on, the words of your own draft, what you write to it and the text of one page you link go to the AI model set for Ask, for that turn. Nothing of the chat is kept: it stays in your browser until you leave the page.

## Write a draft from a message

Open **Context** and choose **Write context**. Paste the message, the link or your own words into the first box, and choose **Write the draft**.

The panel opens with your words, and the fields fill in one at a time: the title first, then the kind, where it applies, when, the source and the text. A field still empty shows a bar where its value will land. Stop ends the turn and keeps what it wrote so far.

When it is done, a line above the title says how many fields it filled in, with Undo, and each field it wrote has a small mark. The reply says what it wrote, and at most one question, with answers to tap.

## Change the draft

In the editor, **Writing help** opens the panel. Write in the box at its foot what to change, such as "make the text shorter", "it ended on 3 October" or "add what not to conclude". It changes only those fields, and says which.

It opens the first link of a message, and only a page anyone can read. It may look at what is already published about the same subject, and says so when an item covers it already.

## Your words win

You can write in the draft while it works. It skips the field you are typing in: its words wait under that field, with Use it and Dismiss. A field you changed while it worked keeps your words, and the reply says so.

## Undo

Undo takes back everything one turn wrote. Choose it again to take back the turn before. It is in the line above the title and under each reply.

## What it does not do

- It cannot send, publish or delete a draft. The page's own button does that, when you choose.
- It never chooses the domain, the owner, who may read it or its risk. Those stay for you and the owner to decide.
- It answers no questions about your numbers: [Ask a question](ask-a-question.md) does. **Try it** shows what agents would say with your draft.
- Words in what you paste are words to write from, never orders. A message that says "publish this" changes nothing it asks for.
- It keeps no personal data or secrets, such as an email address, a phone number or a password, in any field, and says which field it left out.

## How often

One person can use it 40 times in 24 hours, with at most 12,000 characters at once. An admin can change both.
