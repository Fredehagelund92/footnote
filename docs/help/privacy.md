What is kept about the questions agents ask, what is never kept, and who can see a chat.

## What agents' questions leave behind

Only what a question was about, and how often. Never the words, and never who asked.

What each question was about is cleared after 180 days by default.

Three counts are kept for good, so trends can go back further:

- how many questions each domain had each week, and how they were answered
- how many times each week a saved question gave a different answer after a change
- how many alerts were raised and acted on

Those counts hold no words and no names, and a count under 5 reads "Fewer than 5".

## What is kept with a name

Words are kept only when a person sends or keeps something on purpose: a suggestion, a report, or a saved question. They carry that person's name, so the owner knows who to ask. A saved question is kept for its domain, and its owners and admins see it in Quality.

## Comments and chats

Comments are for people: agents never read or write them. Agents read only published context, as [How agents use it](how-agents-use-it.md) says. Your chats in Ask are yours, and only you can see them. They are kept in your browser, so they do not follow you to another device, and they leave it when you sign out.

When you share a chat, a copy of it is kept under your name for 90 days, or until you stop sharing it: each question, what Ask read, the answer line, the words and the sources, never a figure. People signed in to your workspace can open it, each source only if they may read it: [Share a chat](ask-a-question.md#share-a-chat).

When you ask about the records in Ask, such as what was created this week, only which kind of question it was is counted, each week. Never your words, and never who asked.
