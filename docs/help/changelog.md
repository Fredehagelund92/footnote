## The docs show the screens

2026-11-20

Docs pages can now show the screen they are about, in light or dark as you read them. New pages cover [asking a question](ask-a-question.md), [keeping context up to date](keep-context-up-to-date.md), [a question nothing answers](when-nothing-is-written-yet.md) and [what agents asked](see-what-agents-asked.md). [Agent tools](tools.md) is now one page, with what each tool takes and gives back.

## A new name and a new mark

2026-11-18

The app and its docs have a new name and a new mark.

## Agents get the definition in every reply

2026-11-18

When an agent asks about a metric, get_context gives the first sentence of its definition, in the words of the tool it comes from, in the short reply too. An agent that has used its calls for the minute still sees the tools, and a refused call says how many seconds to wait.

## Ask checks each answer before you see it

2026-11-18

Each sentence of an answer is held to what Ask read: its numbers and the items it cites. An answer that fails is written once more, and a sentence that still fails is replaced by a plain one. A sentence no longer loses words where a reference was taken out.

When no AI model is connected, as on the demo, Ask's empty page says so: answers list what your team wrote and an owner checked.

## The Inbox and Ask count the same week

2026-11-18

The Inbox's line for this week counts as Ask's Asked this week does: the last seven full days. Due for a check counts the same items as Context and Settings, so a domain shows one number everywhere.

## Choose the period on the read line

2026-11-17

The days on Ask's read line are a menu: Today, Any date, Last month, Last quarter, Last year and Pick dates. Each asks the same words again for that period.

A follow-up reads its period from your last question, so "and the month before?" moves it back a month. When a month without a year finds different items in two years, Ask asks which year you mean.

## The box sits under the question

2026-11-17

On a wide screen, a new chat has its box under the question, with the states an answer can have below it. The first question moves the box to the bottom. Asked this week opens with a bar of its topics by state.

## Ask says how it read your dates

2026-11-16

The read line says where Ask had to guess, such as "year not given". Words like "since the customs rule" read from the first day of the event of that name, and the read line names the event. Once the workspace sets the day its fiscal year starts, "Q3 FY26" reads as that quarter of the fiscal year.

## Used is one line

2026-11-16

Under an answer, Used is one line: how many items, and how fresh they are. Open it to see each one. An item that is due for a check or out of date stays in sight, with its own Mark it still true.

## Answers arrive a sentence at a time, in your language

2026-11-16

Ask shows each sentence of its answer once it is checked. It answers in the language you ask in and keeps every name as it is written. When nothing is written about a question, Tell the owner sends it to the owners' Inbox.

## Disconnect the agents you connected

2026-11-16

Everyone has Settings now. Agents and access lists the agents you connected, each with Disconnect, so you do not need an admin to take one away.

## The Inbox opens on what is for you

2026-11-15

Mentioned you and Suggestions come before the chores, and every group folds from its header. Due for a check starts folded when it holds more than 10 rows. Its card opens the item's text and names the definition that changed, with the day it changed.

## A suggestion says when the item changed under it

2026-11-14

A suggestion written before its item got a new version says so, and its first action returns it to the person who sent it. An owner can close a suggestion to archive an item, with a reason.

## The app opens on Ask when nothing needs you

2026-11-14

The app opens on the Inbox only when something waits for you, or on a new install's setup. Otherwise it opens on Ask.

## The editor says Saved only when it is

2026-11-13

The editor says Not saved yet until the first save, and leaving with words not saved asks first. A source that is not a link is marked at its field, and the reasons a publish was refused show at their fields.

## Search opens the definition on Enter

2026-11-13

In search, definitions come before context, and Enter opens the first row.

## The demo opens as its admin

2026-11-12

The demo signs you in as its admin. View as, at the top of every page, shows the same company as a domain owner or a reader sees it, without signing out.

## Filter by any dimension: pick it, then its values

2026-11-10

Overview's Filter button, or F, opens one list: pick a field or a dimension, then its values, the ones agents asked about most first, with what each counts, a search, A to Z, and is or is not. Type two letters to find a value in any dimension.

A filter reads in parts, such as Store · is any of · 3 values, and a click on its middle flips is and is not.

Beside the topics, the filter lists keep the fixed fields, one By list you switch between dimensions, and a list for each dimension an admin keeps here for everyone. Your recent dimensions come first in the filter, and stay in your browser.

## Select days on the chart, and drill into a bar

2026-11-03

Drag across Overview's chart, or press B and then Shift and the arrows, to select some days: the numbers add up just those days, the filter bar says which, and a link keeps them. Press a part of a bar to look at only that answer. Escape clears the days.

## Filters as chips, and filter lists with counts

2026-11-03

Overview's filters are chips you add from the bar (press F, or / to type), change in place, move and take off one at a time. A filter can keep a value or leave it out, and you can filter by market and metric too. Filter lists beside the topics show every value with what it counts. A link keeps every filter, and Back undoes the last change.

## Overview and Questions are one page

2026-11-03

Overview now holds the topics too: the numbers, questions per day and the topics follow the same range, domain, kind of agent, view and filters, and a changed number counts to its new value.

## "What is" starts with the definition

2026-11-03

Ask what a metric is, and the answer starts with its definition, in the words of the definitions source. With no context written about it, a Definition mark stands where the answer line would be.

## The review's Preview is the item's page

2026-11-03

In the review, Preview shows the item as its Context page will read once you publish it.

## Emoji in comments

2026-11-03

Type : in a comment to open the emoji list, and letters to narrow it.

## The Inbox pane starts with what agents say

2026-11-03

Select a card in the Inbox to see what agents say today, and what they will say after you decide. The button at the foot is named for the decision, and the other choices are buttons beside it, with their names.

## Observability opens on Overview

2026-11-02

Observability no longer has a Summary page or its monthly digests. It opens on Overview.

## Ask reads like a chat

2026-11-02

The answer line is a badge at the head of the answer, and the badge opens a card that says what it means. Sources are chips under the answer, and its actions are one row of icons. A question that gets nothing back stays in the chat, with Ask again.

## Conflicts are a filter of Context

2026-11-02

Conflicts is a filter of the one Context list, and every item in a conflict has a Conflict chip.

## Ask writes its answer as it goes

2026-11-01

An answer in Ask arrives a sentence at a time, and Esc stops it. Ask takes one question at a time. Ask next is gone: Ask offers questions only after one it could not answer.

## Ask about Definitions

2026-11-01

Ask answers questions about what Definitions lists, such as "list 10 dimensions", "how many metrics" or "what markets are there". When it looks nothing up, it says what it can answer.

## Publish without review

2026-10-31

An owner can let one agent publish low-risk new items in their domain without a review round, once an admin switches it on. Each one names the owner, and can be undone from its history: [Publish without review](publish-without-review.md).

## Compare two items that disagree

2026-10-31

Two context items that disagree open side by side. Keep this one, Keep both, Not a conflict or Ask the owner, each with why. Ask, the Inbox and the review lead to the same view with Compare and decide.

## Every agent has a name

2026-10-31

Every connected agent is listed by its name, with who it works for. An admin switches each of its tools on or off in its Details.

## Comment on a line in the review

2026-10-30

In the review, press C on a line or a field to leave a comment there. A comment can be marked as done, and opened again.

## The review shows the source

2026-10-30

The review opens on Source: each field before and after, and the text with line numbers and the changed words marked. Preview shows the rich text.

## Ask about the context itself

2026-10-30

Ask answers questions about the context's own records: what was created or changed, what is due for a check, what is archived, who owns a domain, how many context items there are, and what agents asked that found nothing.

## Every list sorts both ways

2026-10-30

Choose a sort a second time to turn it around: A to Z becomes Z to A, and Newest first becomes Oldest first.

## Try a figure

2026-10-30

Figures show as the second connection of your definitions source, and Try a figure reads one value to show that the connection works.

## Access keys

2026-10-29

An Admin can make an access key for a bot or a scheduled job in Settings. A key names no person.

## Connect a tool in five steps

2026-10-29

Settings, Integrations connects a tool in five steps, from picking it to its schedule. Change settings goes through the same steps, and Disconnect stops reading it: [Integrations](integrations.md).

## Saved questions and alerts

2026-10-28

A saved question is checked again, and Quality shows whether its answer is the same, changed or needs a look. An alert rule raises a card in the owner's Inbox.

## Documents become suggestions

2026-10-28

Connect Markdown files in Git, or Google Drive, as a content source, and its documents arrive as suggestions to review.

## Related items

2026-10-28

A context page lists the items related to it, and opens one beside the page.

## Sign in with your company's account

2026-10-27

People can sign in with the company's OpenID Connect provider. An Admin invites people and gives them the Admin or Member role.

## Help in one place

2026-10-23

One Help menu in the sidebar, a short Help page, a card on every answer line, and ? for the keys.

## Agents can follow links

2026-10-23

An agent can follow a link between context items, definitions and domains, one step at a time. Related shows the same.

## Synonyms are tags

2026-10-22

Type other names for a definition and a comma; a word that already means something else says who has it.

## Event, Warning, Rule and Guide

2026-10-22

The four kinds of context have short, plain names.

## The review waits while you type

2026-10-21

Single keys never act in a note, offline publishing waits, and Publish the ones you've read lists them first.

## Connect an agent with one prompt

2026-10-20

A live line shows waiting, then Connected, with the agent's name and yours.
