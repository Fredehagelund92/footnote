## Writing help opens beside a new item

2026-11-28

Write context now opens the editor at once, with writing help beside it and the cursor in its box: paste a message or a link, or say what happened, and the draft fills in beside you. The page that asked What happened? first is gone. Close the panel, and new items open without it in your browser until you open it again.

Write context under a Not found answer in Ask, and a Nothing written yet card in the Inbox, open writing help with what they found. Under an Outdated answer, and on a Needs a recheck card, Update it opens the item's editor with writing help asking what changed. The arrow in writing help's box is called Say it, so Send is only the page's own button. Draft from text, in the editor's More menu, still fills the fields by rule, with no AI model.

## Plainer names for three things

2026-11-28

An item whose check date has passed, or whose definition changed, is now marked Needs a recheck, and the reminder to its owner is A recheck is due. In the Context list, the button that confirms the items you opened is now Confirm again.

The Inbox card for a question nothing is written for is called Nothing written yet everywhere, including this help. Not found stays the name of an answer line and of an alert.

Ask and your agent's tools use the new names too, and nothing you stored changed.

## A definition's page leads with what it means

2026-11-28

A definition's page shows what it means first, in large words, with one line on where the words come from. Its other names are one line under them, with Add a name.

A metric's Built from tree stays open. Variants, How it is built, Where it comes from and History are closed rows, each with a few words on what it holds. A family's page reads its values in the sentence, such as in EUR or USD, and lists up to ten variants one a line. See [Definitions and synonyms](definitions-and-synonyms.md#a-definitions-page).

## A source with no link

2026-11-28

What lives in someone's head or a chat thread has no page to link to. In the editor's Source, choose No link and write one line that says who or what says so, such as Pricing committee, 20 August 2026. The item can then be published, and agents are told there is no link to open. Your agent can write such a source too.

## Who you are, on your own data

2026-11-28

An install on your own data asks the first person who opens it for their name, their email and the workspace's name, and makes them its admin. It has no sample people.

## The first questions agents could not answer

2026-11-28

While fewer than five people have signed in, each question an agent finds nothing for shows in the owner's Inbox at once, as a Nothing written yet card. Later the usual count applies: 5 in 7 days.

## What to write next

2026-11-28

After the setup checklist, the Inbox keeps a Next card until five context items are published: the metrics nothing is written about yet, the ones agents asked about first, and where to find more of what your team knows.

## An agent writes many drafts, and sends none

2026-11-27

Your agent can turn a page of your wiki into context in one go: it writes each item as its own draft and sends none. They wait on one card in your Inbox, Your drafts, where you read them one after another and publish them together.

Your agents can now save 50 new drafts in 24 hours, with 50 waiting, up from 10 and 20. An admin can change both. See [Agent tools](tools.md).

## Whole words in Context by time and in wide tables

2026-11-27

In Context by time the line for today runs under the bars and their titles, not through them. A title whose bar starts before the left edge of the screen keeps its first words in view and ends in "...", on a phone too.

On a phone, and wherever a table is too wide for the page, Advanced and an integration's page show each row as a block: a setting's key, its value and what it does, or a folder with its domain, owners and documents. Nothing is cut mid-word and nothing scrolls sideways.

## Write context with your agent

2026-11-27

Write context now offers your own agent beside Fill it in. Write with Claude opens Claude's app on a new chat with a prompt ready: Claude writes the item with you as your draft, and you change the same draft in the app. Another agent you connected gets the prompt copied. It is also under a Not found answer and in the editor's More menu.

Your agent reads the draft again before each change, so it keeps your words. It looks again when you write to it, not while you type. In Claude Code, the same prompt is /footnote:write_context. [Write with your agent](write-with-your-agent.md) has the steps, and the line to keep in a Claude Project.

## A first item that is never lost, and nothing to guess

2026-11-27

A new owner's first hour lost words, or left them to guess. Now:

- A new context item saves itself as your draft once it has a title or a sentence. A reload loses nothing.
- When says how each choice differs, offers the date your text names, and takes a date you type.
- Don't conclude, left empty, adds nothing to what agents get. When your text gives a cause, the field says so.
- Every ready name on the Names card can be renamed before it is published, and short forms such as MRR keep their capitals.
- A definition with no description says where to add one in its tool.
- `SEED_DEMO=off docker compose up` starts with no demo and no definitions.

See [Write context](write-context.md).

## Metrics kept apart, and made one family later

2026-11-27

Metrics you keep separate on the Names card are now listed in Definitions, under Kept apart in the Families view, beside the families split since. An owner of their domain, or an admin, can make them one family there. A source's page in Integrations lists them too.

A family's History now names the person who published it, split it or made it one again, not the tool that reads its metrics. Delete on a family says to split it on its page instead.

## Counts in the thousands on a source's page

2026-11-27

A source's page in Integrations wrote counts in the thousands without a comma, such as "5062 definitions read". Its numbers, its activity and its list of what it reads now write 5,062.

## A new workspace says only what is so

2026-11-27

A first hour on a new install met screens that said something that was not so, or nothing at all. They now say what is so:

- After the demo, a start on your own definitions stops and says how to start empty, so the two are never mixed.
- Start from the guide, in About our business, gives the shape of the text to fill in, not the demo company's words.
- With no AI model connected, Ask gives each item's whole summary, and an admin sees how to connect a model.
- The Inbox says agents asked today on the day the first one asks.
- Agents and access counts the agents connected already, and a Domain field says when there is no domain yet.
- Invite people says when someone invited cannot sign in yet.
- A file address on the server itself that cannot be reached says why, and a key can be shown as you type it.

See [Set up your workspace](set-up-your-workspace.md).

## Tidier pages on a tablet and a phone

2026-11-27

A person's small round picture shows their initials, so the View as button on a phone and the author of a term in Definitions read as people. Definitions shows a term's marks in one column.

In Ask the sources under an answer are one list, in the order of their numbers, each on one line with the day it was checked, or what to do about it when it needs a look.

On a tablet an item waiting for review keeps its title in the top bar, and its other choices are in More. The documentation's top bar fits a tablet's width. On a phone a button beside a few words, such as a setup step's, sits under them.

## A phone's width keeps what a page holds

2026-11-27

A window made narrow enough to show a phone's layout started the page again, so a tested connection, and anything typed but not yet saved, was gone. The page now keeps it at every width.

## Room on a tablet, and buttons a finger can hit

2026-11-27

In a window under 1024 px wide the sidebar starts as its row of icons, so the Inbox, Ask and the lists have room on a tablet. The button at the top left opens it again.

The top bar of a page folds when it is narrow: its buttons show their icons before a title is cut short. On a touch screen the buttons in the top bar are the same size on every page, with room around each for a finger.

On a phone a side panel keeps all its buttons on the screen, the Context list gives its titles more room, and the table that compares a summary with its source fits the screen.

## One definitions tool at a time

2026-11-26

One definitions tool is on at a time, so Definitions lists each metric once. To switch, turn off the tool that is on in Integrations, then connect or turn on the other. Its definitions leave Definitions, and context that relies on one is marked until it is on again. See [Integrations](integrations.md#one-definitions-tool-at-a-time).

## Publishing many drafts says what happened

2026-11-26

Publish on selected drafts in Context shows "Publishing 50 drafts..." while it works, and its buttons wait until it is done. Drafts that were already published, by you a moment before, say "already published" instead of failing.

## Families with the same name are told apart

2026-11-26

Definitions listed the same name many times when several families shared it, such as Forecast Revenue. A read now gives each family a distinct name, and the families that already shared one were renamed once, with the change recorded in Settings history. See [Metric variants](metric-variants.md).

## Publish moves on at once

2026-11-25

Publish in a review opens the next suggestion at once. The one you left says Publishing, then Published once it is in, or why it couldn't be. Command and Z undoes your last decision from any screen, for as long as Undo is offered. See [Review a suggestion](review-a-suggestion.md#after-you-decide).

## The keys work on every list

2026-11-25

J and K move through Context, Definitions and the Inbox from the moment the page opens, and the row you move to takes the focus. With a side panel open, J opens the next row in it while the panel keeps the focus, and Esc closes the panel from anywhere. After a click on an Inbox card, one Enter opens it.

G then a letter goes to a place: I the Inbox, A Ask, C Context, D Definitions and S Settings. Press ? to see every key.

## See what a metric is built from

2026-11-25

A metric built from others shows them as a small tree, on its page and in the side panel, with the metrics they are built from in turn. Each line counts the published context about that metric, so you can see where something is written. See [Definitions](definitions-and-synonyms.md#what-a-metric-is-built-from).

## Lists keep their view, and Back goes back

2026-11-25

Context and Definitions keep their filters, search words, sort and grouping in the address, so a reload, or Back from an item's page, gives the same list with the row you came from in focus.

Moving through the Inbox with J and K no longer adds a Back step, so leaving the Inbox takes one Back. Esc, or the back arrow, on something opened from a list goes back to that list.

## Share a chat as a link

2026-11-25

Share chat, at the top of Ask, makes one link to a copy of the whole chat, under your name. Only people signed in can open it, each source only if they may read it, and it lasts 90 days. Stop sharing is in History. An answer no longer has a Share of its own. See [Share a chat](ask-a-question.md#share-a-chat).

## Test a tool from its page

2026-11-24

Test connection is on every definitions tool's page now, under Connection, a tool set up on the server included. It tests the tool as it is saved, names each file a read skips, and says what to do when something fails. Families are always on the page too, with how to make one in your tool.

## Figures leave Ask, for now

2026-11-24

Ask no longer shows a number read from your definitions tool, and its page in Integrations has one connection again. Your workspace keeps no figures: an answer says what is written about a number, and the number itself is in the tool that holds your data.

## Drafts are yours alone

2026-11-24

Only the person who wrote a draft can see it now. Owners and admins see it once it is sent, as Waiting. The editor has Save as draft, and a draft's page sends or publishes it as it is.

Drafts you imported, or haven't saved for 30 days, wait on one card in your Inbox, which publishes or sends them all at once. An admin can delete the drafts of someone who left, without reading them. See [Write context](write-context.md#save-as-draft).

## Five tools for agents

2026-11-24

Agents now call five tools. Given no words, find_context lists what is defined and written down, a page at a time, with each item's summary. read_context gives an item's links with every read, so Agents and access no longer has a Let agents follow links switch, and Settings lists five tools.

Only get_context says to call it first, and only for a question about a metric, so an agent looks a name up with find_context. In Claude Code, get_context loads when a session starts. See [Agent tools](tools.md).

## The period you chose, in the answer

2026-11-24

When you choose other dates in Read as, the new answer says so: its Read as names the period with its days, such as last month with its first and last day, and its first sentence names it. The answer above keeps its own dates. An answer about what a metric means offers no dates, since they do not change it. See [Ask a question](ask-a-question.md#ask-again-at-other-dates).

## Waiting for review, and Don't publish

2026-11-23

A new item someone sent now shows in Context as Waiting, with who sent it, how long ago and who decides, to its sender, its domain's owners and admins. Filter by Waiting for review to see them all.

On its page an owner can publish it, edit it and then publish it, send it back with a note, or choose Don't publish with a reason. The sender reads who closed it and why in their Inbox. See [Review a suggestion](review-a-suggestion.md#from-context).

## Families where you connect a source

2026-11-23

Test connection now leads with the families a read would find: how many of the metrics are variants, and how each family was found, declared in your tool, from the calculation, from a rate or by names that look alike. A family found from a rate is now its own kind.

The Names card has a Families part apart from the Names, and a source's page counts its families with Review families. See [Variants](metric-variants.md#what-test-connection-says).

## Buttons that start with what you asked for

2026-11-22

The buttons that open a screen now fill in what they are about. Add the word to Definitions, under an answer that found a word nobody defined, opens a new term with that word. A definition card with no context yet offers its owners Write context.

In the Inbox, a report from Ask shows the question asked, with Try this question, and a card that needs a recheck because a definition changed has See the definition. See [Keep context up to date](keep-context-up-to-date.md#reported).

## Ask opens the screen it explains

2026-11-22

An answer in Ask that says how to do something in the app, such as writing a context item or inviting a colleague, now ends with a button that opens that screen. When the screen is not yours to use, the answer says who does it instead. See [Ask a question](ask-a-question.md#ask-how-it-works).

## Help pages open their screen

2026-11-22

Every help page about a screen now says where to start and has a button beside its title that opens that screen, such as New context on [Write context](write-context.md#add-a-context-item).

## Families declared in your tool

2026-11-21

A family can now be declared in your tool, under each variant's `meta.footnote`, and the Names card marks it declared in your tool.

A metric in USD worked out from its EUR twin is a family by Currency when its rate column converts other pairs too, and inside a family its page shows a Converted row, such as USD from EUR by the column. Metrics whose names alone look alike wait under Look alike, to publish as one family or apart. See [Variants](metric-variants.md#declare-a-family-in-your-tool).

## What a metric is built from

2026-11-21

A metric's page now says what it is built from, read from your dbt project: a ratio's two metrics and a derived metric's inputs, each a link, and the columns its calculation reads. Each input says which metrics use it. See [Definitions](definitions-and-synonyms.md#what-a-metric-is-built-from).

## Families of metrics

2026-11-20

A model with a metric for each currency and channel now shows each set of variants as one family, such as Revenue with 250 variants. The Names card publishes a family with one press, Definitions lists it once with a family mark, and a search for a variant finds its family.

Context about a family applies to every variant, and needs a recheck once when any of them changes. See [Variants](metric-variants.md#if-you-keep-a-metric-for-each-variant).

## Light and dark in the docs

2026-11-20

The Docs area has the theme control at the end of its header, as the rest of the app does, and keeps the same choice. Each page names the version it describes, with a link to read the latest on the web.

## A metric for each currency

2026-11-20

Test connection says when many metrics look like variants of a few, such as a metric for each currency, and links to [a page on modelling them as a dimension](metric-variants.md). It also reads a dbt file of up to 20 MB, and says how large a larger one is.

One context item can apply to hundreds of metrics and publishes in one step. When a question names no metric, agents are offered the metrics its words name. Definitions stays quick at thousands of rows, and the Inbox shows what needs a recheck before setup is done.

## The docs show the screens

2026-11-20

Docs pages can now show the screen they are about, in light or dark as you read them. New pages cover [asking a question](ask-a-question.md), [keeping context up to date](keep-context-up-to-date.md), [a question nothing answers](when-nothing-is-written-yet.md) and [what agents asked](see-what-agents-asked.md). [Agent tools](tools.md) is now one page, with what each tool takes and gives back.

## A new name and a new mark

The app and its docs have a new name and a new mark.

## Agents get the definition in every reply

2026-11-18

When an agent asks about a metric, get_context gives the first sentence of its definition, in the words of the tool it comes from, in the short reply too. An agent that has used its calls for the minute still sees the tools, and a refused call says how many seconds to wait.

## Ask checks each answer before you see it

2026-11-18

Each sentence of an answer is held to what Ask read: its numbers and the items it cites. An answer that fails is written once more, and a sentence that still fails is replaced by a plain one. A sentence no longer loses words where a reference was taken out.

When no AI model is connected, as on the demo, Ask's empty page says so: answers list what your team wrote and an owner checked.

## The Inbox and Ask count the same week

2026-11-18

The Inbox's line for this week counts as Ask's Asked this week does: the last seven full days. Needs a recheck counts the same items as Context and Settings, so a domain shows one number everywhere.

## Choose the period on the read line

2026-11-17

The days on Ask's read line are a menu: Today, Any date, Last month, Last quarter, Last year and Pick dates. Each asks the same words again for that period.

A follow-up reads its period from your last question, so "and the month before?" moves it back a month. When a month without a year finds different items in two years, Ask asks which year you mean.

## The box sits under the question

2026-11-17

On a wide screen, a new chat has its box under the question, with the states an answer can have below it. The first question moves the box to the bottom. Asked this week opens with a bar of its topics by state.

## Ask says how it read your dates

2026-11-16

The read line says where Ask had to guess, such as "year not given". Words like "since the customs rule" read from the first day of the event of that name, and the read line names the event. Once the workspace sets the day its fiscal year starts, "Q3 FY26" reads as that quarter of the fiscal year.

## Used is one line

2026-11-16

Under an answer, Used is one line: how many items, and how fresh they are. Open it to see each one. An item that needs a recheck or is out of date stays in sight, with its own Mark it still true.
