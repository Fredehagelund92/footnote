A suggestion waits in its owner's Inbox. The quick view says what changes; the review is where you decide.

## The quick view

Select a suggestion in the Inbox to see, side by side, what agents say today and what they will say after you publish. It also shows who sent it, and anything that needs care, such as details to compare with the source.

![The Inbox with a suggestion open beside the list: Units sold excludes test accounts, sent by Ines Duarte from a chat. For the question How many test accounts does units sold leave out in Norway?, Agents say today and After you publish are side by side, both Checked, with the changed words underlined. Review is at the foot.](../images/docs/inbox-light.webp)

- A suggestion that clashes with live context shows what agents would hear if you publish it as it is.
- A suggestion to archive shows what they will say after you archive.

What changes and the sender's note are folded below. Open them when you want them.

The button at the foot opens the full screen and says what you decide there:

- Review
- Compare with what's published, for a suggestion about the same thing as published context
- Check the first version, for a draft of what agents could not answer

The words beside it say what follows. The other buttons say what they do, such as Edit or Archive.

## The review

![The review of Ines Duarte's suggestion to raise the test-account count from 312 to 340. Fields shows the source as it is now beside the suggested one, with the change marked. Beside it, Try it and decide shows what agents will say, with Send back with a note and Publish.](../images/docs/review-light.webp)

- The change, as agents will read it: the fields that change as a table, then the text with what was taken out and what was added. N and P step through the changes.
- Preview: the item as its page will read once you publish it. The review keeps to the source or the preview as you go through the waiting suggestions.
- Try it: what agents will say with the draft, and what they say now.
- The Ready line, which never says more than was checked: new numbers in an edit read Ready, not compared with a source.
- Comments on a line or a field: point at a line of the text or a row of the fields and choose the + beside it, or press C, or tap it on a phone. The comment hangs there.

  Type @ to mention someone, or : for an emoji. Anyone who reads the suggestion can mark it done, or open it again, and the comment says who did and when. The side panel counts the open ones and goes to each. Open comments never hold Publish.

- Publish, or Send back with a note: the note is the first comment, and the sender answers there. It waits in their Inbox under Returned to you, where Edit and send again opens it in the editor. A suggestion to archive is archived or closed, not returned.
- Don't publish closes a new item or a change you don't want. Write why: its sender reads the reason in their Inbox, under Closed. Agents never get it.
- A suggestion written against an earlier version of its item, because the item was checked or changed since, says so. Return it, so its sender writes it against what agents get now.
- A suggestion to archive you don't want: choose Don't archive and say why. Nothing changes, and the reason is kept with the decision.
- A suggestion to archive that its sender took back in the comments shows their words. Close the suggestion and nothing changes, or archive anyway, which asks first.

## After you decide

Publish opens the next suggestion at once. In the list of waiting suggestions, the one you left says Publishing, then Published once it is in. If it couldn't be published, it says Not published and why, and nothing changed. Agents get it only once it says Published.

Every decision shows a note with Undo, which opens that suggestion again. Command and Z undoes your last decision from any page, for as long as Undo is offered.

## From Context

A new item waiting for review is in Context too, chipped Waiting. Its page has Publish, Edit, Send back with a note and Don't publish. Edit opens it in the editor, and Publish there publishes it with your changes, its sender still its writer.

## Taking back a suggestion to archive

Suggested archiving a context item, and changed your mind? Say why in its comments, in your Inbox under All, then choose Withdraw the suggestion under your comment. The owner sees your words in the review and closes it, or archives anyway. Keep the suggestion under the same comment lets it stand again.

## When two items disagree

In the review, an owner can say that a suggestion disagrees with a live item. The two are then in a conflict, and agents give both until someone decides it: [Decide a conflict](decide-a-conflict.md).

## Publishing several

Publish the ones you've read takes only suggestions you read: the whole change on screen for a few seconds, or Mark as read. It lists them first, and Undo puts them all back.

An owner can also let one named agent publish some of its suggestions without a review: [Publish without review](publish-without-review.md).

> **Keys.** J and K move through the waiting suggestions, N and P through the changes, the arrows through the lines, C comments on the one in focus, Command and Enter publishes, Command and Z undoes the last decision, Esc goes back to the Inbox, and ? lists them all.
