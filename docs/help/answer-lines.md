Every answer, in the app and in your own agent, starts with one of six lines. Each says what the answer rests on, and what it asks of you.

## The six lines

<!-- include answer-lines -->

| The line                                            | What it means                                                                                                  | What you do                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Checked: This is written down and checked.          | Based on checked context. Context applies, and its owner published it with a source and a check date.          | Nothing to do.                                  |
| Partial: Only part of this is written down.         | Covers part of it. Context covers part of the question, or the dates were assumed. The answer says which part. | Write the missing part, or tell the owner.      |
| Not found: Nothing is written about this yet.       | Nothing written down. Nobody has written anything down for this. It does not mean there is no explanation.     | Write context, or tell the owner.               |
| Conflict: Two items say different things.           | Context disagrees. Two items that apply say different things. Agents give both and say so.                     | An owner keeps one, narrows one, or keeps both. |
| Outdated: Some of this may be old.                  | May be out of date. An item's check date has passed, or a definition it relies on changed.                     | The owner checks it: Still true, or Edit.       |
| Not looked up: Ask could not look this up just now. | Could not look up. Ask could not look this up just now. That does not mean nothing is written.                 | Ask again in a minute.                          |

<!-- /include -->

A question that names no metric and no market is not looked up, so its answer has none of the six lines. Ask says "Nothing was looked up", says why, and names the kinds of question it answers: about a metric or a market, about the records, and about what Definitions lists. Not found is only for a question that was looked up and found nothing.

A question about the context itself, such as "what was created in the last 24 hours?", "what is due for a check in Sales?" or "who owns Orders?", gets an answer from the records instead. It starts with a Records chip, not one of the six lines: it counts what you can see in Context, and agents never read it.

Ask also reads the records to say what is missing, out of date, in conflict or without an owner, such as "what definitions are missing?" or "what is waiting on me?". Every count there comes with the total it is out of, and the answer says what it cannot see.

- A topic agents asked about only a few times is not shown.
- A question beyond what is written, such as "should we restart the Norway campaign?", gets a fixed sentence saying so, followed by what is written.
- Ask does not give advice of its own.
- Ask keeps no numbers, so for numbers use an agent with your data tool.

Each line names what the person can do next: [Write context](write-context.md) says how to write the missing part. The words the lines use, such as owner and context item, are on [Words](words.md).

> **One list.** Help in the app shows the same lines, from the same source. If one changes, both change.
