When agents keep asking about something and find nothing written down, the answer says Not found instead of guessing. The question then comes back to the owners as a card in their Inbox, under Nothing written yet, so the next answer can be Checked.

At the foot are Not something we write down and Check the first version.](../images/docs/inbox-nothing-written-light.webp)

## What raises a card

A card is about one topic: a metric, the values asked about, such as a market, and the month. It comes when agents' questions about it find nothing 5 or more times in 7 days, a number an admin can change in Alerts. While fewer than five people have signed in, it comes at the first question that finds nothing, or that an agent reports as wrong or missing: on a workspace that new, a count points to nobody you don't already know. When someone presses Tell the owner on a Not found answer in Ask, the card says so: [Ask a question](ask-a-question.md#when-nothing-is-written).

A word someone told an admin about from Ask, because no metric or context item uses it, comes to the admins as a card titled with that word.

The owners of the topic's domain get it, and admins. A topic that spans domains reaches the owners of each.

## What the card shows

- How many times agents found nothing for it this week. Under 5, it says fewer than 5 times, so a count never gives a person away.
- Whether people asked about it in Ask too.
- What agents asked, under What agents asked, and where the fix probably belongs, such as context about the topic.
- A draft, if someone already wrote context for it: Agents say today beside After you publish shows what changes once it is published.

## Answer it

| The card               | Press                              | What happens                                                                                           |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Has no draft           | **Write context**                  | The editor opens on the topic. When you publish, the card closes, and agents get the item from then on |
| You don't know         | **Ask someone who knows**          | A link to one question is copied, for you to paste to the person who knows                             |
| Has a draft            | Check the first version, or Review | The review opens on the draft. Compare each detail with its source, then publish it                    |
| Is not for the context | **Not something we write down**    | The card closes, and agents keep saying honestly that nothing is written down                          |

Write the item as [Write context](write-context.md) says, and [review a draft](review-a-suggestion.md) as any suggestion.

## Ask someone who knows

Often the person who knows what happened is not you. Press **Ask someone who knows** and paste the link wherever your company talks. Nothing is sent for you: the card only says the day you copied the link, never who opened it.

The link opens one page with one question. It shows the topic and its dates, never the words anyone asked. The person writes what happened in a sentence or two, changes the dates if needed, and adds a link to where it is written if they have one. What they send is a suggestion under their name, and it waits for you like any other.

They may send it without a link. Then you add a source before you publish, or confirm the link they gave by copying a sentence from it: the review says Add a source first, or Confirm the source first, until you do. Nothing they wrote reaches an agent before you publish.

Only people who can sign in can answer. With company sign-in, an admin can map a company group so that colleagues sign in without an invite. Anyone else is told to ask the person who sent the link.

## Undo

Undo is offered for a few seconds as the card closes. A card you answered is under All, as Done. If a publish that covered a card is undone, the card comes back.

Once an item covers the topic, a question about it is answered from that item. If the item ever needs a look, its card is under Due for a check: [Keep context up to date](keep-context-up-to-date.md).
